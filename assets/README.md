# Demo assets

Drop a cold-open clip here as `hero.mp4` (or `hero.webm`) and it plays
full-bleed behind the title card at `/hero`, then leads into the console.

The page is designed to work **without** one — the typographic form is the real
design and the video is an upgrade. A hero that breaks without its asset is a
hero that breaks on stage.

Clips are gitignored: they are demo assets, not source, and they are large.
