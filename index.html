Recoup — evidence

bounded revenue recovery · razorpay ai buildathon · track 03

Recoup detects a failed recurring charge, decides what is worth doing, and cannot take a money action its policy engine has not permitted. These are the records it produces. Nothing here is a control surface: there is nothing to trigger and no state to change.

One case, all the way through

Detect → Diagnose → Intervene → Recover → Measure
One case walked from the verified provider delivery that opened it to the money that came back, with every gate evaluation on the way. The loop closes on a payment, not a chart.

The audit report

Full run, every case
Case timelines, model proposals, refusal traces and the measured results, in one self-contained file.

Compliance attestations

One per case, built from the append-only ledger. Six checks, each answering a question the ledger settles — and each able to come back negative. A report that can only say “pass” attests to nothing.

These all read no exceptions, which is worth one sentence of explanation. They did not always. This report is what found that a case had sent 39 pre-debit notices and executed no debits — every one individually permitted, because a statutory notice is exempt from the cooldown by design and nothing else counted them. A ninth policy gate now caps them, and the check that found the hole was rewritten to audit that gate rather than repeat its rule: it can now only fire if the gate failed.
